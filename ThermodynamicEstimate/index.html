    Class: thermodynamic estimate (ThermodynamicEstimate)

    A reified thermodynamic quantity for a reaction, bundled with the aqueous conditions it holds at, its uncertainty, and the method and source it came from. Transformed ("primed") quantities are only interpretable together with pH, ionic strength, pMg and temperature, because the transform aggregates each reactant's microspecies into a single pseudoisomer group at those conditions. A reaction may therefore carry several estimates, one per condition set.
